## Host Interface

!!! warning "Plugging ONT sticks into a NIC is discouraged"
    NICs have been known to under-power the WAS-110 and cause it to enter O7 (emergency stop). Other ONT sticks with
    the same [architecture](#architecture) may be affected too. A media converter or network switch port is
    recommended instead.

    Not all NIC and ONT stick combinations have been tested. To check your own, please read the
    [Troubleshoot connectivity issues with the WAS-110] guide's [Supply voltage] section.
